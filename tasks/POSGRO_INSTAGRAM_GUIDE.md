## 2. Minimalist visual system

Minimalism only works if it's **systematic**. Lock these rules once and never deviate — consistency is what reads as "professional brand" on a grid.

### 2.1 Fixed rules
| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Background | White `#FFFFFF` or off-white `#F5F5F5` only |
| Accent | Brand blue `#1976d2` for primary, pink `#dc004e` sparingly for one highlight per post |
| Gradient | Reserved for the logo and cover moments only — never behind body text |
| Type | One sans-serif (Roboto or Inter). Bold for headline, regular for body. |
| Photos | Real, well-lit, uncluttered. One subject per frame. |
| Text on image | Max 6–8 words. Large. Lots of empty space. |
| Borders/stickers | None. No drop shadows, no emojis inside graphics. |

### 2.2 The 3-lane grid pattern
Plan the feed so every third post is the same "type," creating a rhythm when someone lands on your profile:

- **Lane A** — App/product screenshot on clean background (you have these).
- **Lane B** — Real photo of the POS running in a store (you have these).
- **Lane C** — Single-fact text card (blue on white).

Posting in A→B→C→A→B→C order keeps the grid visually balanced with zero design skill required.

### 2.3 Free tools
- **Canva** (free): build **one** reusable template per lane, then only swap the photo/text. Set the template to **1080×1350 px (4:5 portrait)** — it takes more vertical space in the feed and gets more reach than square.
- Keep a Canva folder of the 3 master templates so posting is copy-swap-export.

## 4. Posting cadence (realistic for solo, self-shot)

Do not overcommit — an abandoned account hurts the brand. This schedule is sustainable alone:

| Type | Frequency | Effort |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Feed post (4:5) | **3× per week** (e.g. Mon / Wed / Fri) | Swap Canva template, post |
| Stories | **2–3× per week** | Behind-the-scenes, phone-shot, 15s |
| Reels | **1× per week** once you have clips | 10–20s screen recording or store clip |

**Best local posting window:** evenings **19:00–22:00 Asia/Tashkent**, when store owners are off the floor. Test and adjust using Insights after 2 weeks.

> You don't have a demo video yet. Easiest first Reels: (a) a **screen recording** of the app doing one action — scan → cart → receipt; (b) a **10-second clip** of a real checkout. No editing needed beyond trimming.

## 5. Production workflow (phone only)

1. **Photos of the POS in-store** (you have these): shoot in landscape-free, straight-on, natural light off. One clear subject. Wipe the screen first — glare kills minimalist shots.
2. **App screenshots** (you have these): drop onto a white Canva frame, add one 6-word O'zbek headline. Done.
3. **Text cards**: Canva Lane-C template, change the sentence only.
4. **Stories**: shoot vertical, add nothing but maybe one line of O'zbek text. Raw and real outperforms polished here.
5. Batch **one week (3 posts) in a single sitting** so you're never scrambling.

## 6. Boosting on under $30/month

With this budget, **do not open full Ads Manager**. Use in-app **Boost** — simpler, and enough for city-level brand awareness.

### 6.1 Payment setup (Uzbekistan-specific — read first)
- Meta bills in **USD** and needs a card that supports **international** payments.
- A **UzCard/Humo local-only card will not work.** Use one of:
  1. A **dollar Visa/Mastercard** from an Uzbek bank (most issue one — request international payments enabled).
  2. A **virtual USD Visa** (fintech/crypto-funded) as a fallback — lets you cap spend to exactly your budget.
- Add it in **Settings → Payments/Billing** before your first boost.
- At under $30/month you are **well below** Meta's high-spend threshold, so the April 2026 credit-card removal does **not** affect you — a normal card is fine.

### 6.2 What to boost
- **Never boost text-card posts.** Boost only **Lane B** (real store photo) or a **Reel** — real, human content earns cheaper reach.
- Boost your **1 best-performing organic post per week** (check Insights for highest organic reach), not a fresh one.

### 6.3 Boost settings for brand awareness
| Setting | Choice |
|---------|--------|
| Goal | **More profile visits** or **More reach/views** (awareness) — NOT "More messages" |
| Audience | **Create your own** (not "Automatic") |
| Location | **Fergana** + 15–25 km radius. Add Farg'ona / Marg'ilon / Qo'qon if relevant. |
| Age | 25–55 |
| Interests | Small business, Entrepreneurship, Retail, Point of sale, Grocery store |
| Duration | 5–6 days |
| Budget | ~$4–5 per boost |

### 6.4 Monthly $30 split
- **4 boosts × ~$5** = ~$20 → steady weekly awareness.
- Hold back **~$8–10** for the one post that's clearly over-performing organically — extend it 2–3 more days.
- Keep the last **~$0–2** as buffer for currency/fees.

## 9. What to measure (awareness, not vanity)

Ignore likes. For brand awareness track weekly in **Insights**:

| Metric | Why it matters | Healthy direction |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Reach** | How many distinct people saw you | Up week-over-week |
| **Profile visits** | Interest beyond the scroll | Up after boosts |
| **Follower growth** | Compounding audience | Steady, organic |
| **Accounts reached from ads** | Boost efficiency | Cost per 1k reach falling |
| **Saves / shares** | Content that resonates | Any is good for B2B |

Review every Sunday. Kill content types that get low reach; double down on what performs.

---

## 10. Minimalism guardrails (do / don't)

**Do:** white space, one message per post, one accent color, real photos, short O'zbek captions, consistent 4:5 grid.

**Don't:** stock photos of foreign supermarkets, cluttered collages, more than 2 fonts, rainbow of colors, emoji stuffing inside graphics, walls of text, engagement-bait ("tag 3 friends").
